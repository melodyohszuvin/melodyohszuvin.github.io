Roy Wang



Roy Wang (Wang Yuan) is a famous singer, songwriter, and actor from China. He became very popular as a young teenager when he joined the famous pop group TFBOYS. Roy loves music very much and writes many of his own songs. To learn more about music, he went to study at Berklee College of Music in the United States. He has released many popular songs and albums as a solo artist, and he has held big concerts for his fans.
